Questions and Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to popular questions about enrollment and training

BAYUN training centers are located in the Moscow, Kursk, and Tyumen regions of the Russian Federation. We provide comfortable living and training conditions.
Our goal is to provide every candidate with specialized training under highly qualified instructors, Russian language learning, and building confidence in using specialized equipment.
You will be accommodated in comfortable barracks (1-4 people per room). Three meals a day, 4-5 hours of daily Russian language study, and specialized training following Russian special services programs.
After signing the contract, you go to the military unit for enrollment and receiving allowance. This process takes 3-5 days. After enrollment is confirmed, the Ministry of Defense of the Russian Federation transfers 2,600,000 rubles to your Russian bank card. Then monthly — from 210,000 rubles.
You need to provide test results for HIV, hepatitis and absence of prohibited substances, as well as passport scans. We will verify the documents within 3-4 days and begin the process of obtaining entry to Russia.
Yes. According to Decree of the President of the Russian Federation No. 821 of November 5, 2025, you receive Russian citizenship three months after signing the contract and beginning military service.
